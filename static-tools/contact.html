Frequently Asked Questions

Are all tools really free?

Yes! All tools on Tools Hub are completely free to use. There's no signup required, no premium version, and no hidden fees.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text, images, and passwords never leave your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Of course! We'd love to hear your suggestions. Use the contact form above and select "Feature Suggestion" as the subject.

How does Tools Hub make money?

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. This allows us to keep all tools free while covering hosting and development costs.
