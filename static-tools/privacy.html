Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2024

Information We Collect

Tools Hub is designed with your privacy in mind. Our utility tools process data directly in your browser, which means:

  • Text Utils: All text you enter is processed locally and never transmitted to any server.
  • Password Generator: Passwords are generated entirely in your browser. We do not store, transmit, or log any generated passwords.
  • BMI Calculator: Height and weight values are used only for calculation within your browser.
  • FF Nickname Generator: Nicknames are generated locally without any data collection.
  • Meme Generator: Your text is rendered locally. No images or text are stored on our servers.
  • Quiz App: Quiz interactions happen entirely in your browser session.
  • Image Compressor: Images are processed locally using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect the following information when you visit Tools Hub:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website addresses
  • Date and time of your visit

This information is collected through standard server logs and is used solely for website analytics and improvement purposes.

Cookies and Advertising

Essential Cookies: We use essential cookies to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) which is stored in your browser's localStorage.

Advertising Cookies: We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

Tools Hub uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Google Fonts: For typography. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to maintaining reasonable protections for your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal information we may have collected about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings

Children's Privacy

Tools Hub does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
